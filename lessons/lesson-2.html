Lesson 2: Headings & Paragraphs

Time: 5 min | Goal: Structure text like a real article

Heading Hierarchy: h1 to h6

HTML has 6 heading levels. <h1> is most important, <h6> is least. Think of them like document outline:

<h1>Main Title</h1>
<h2>Section</h2>
<h3>Subsection</h3>
<p>Regular paragraph text</p>
SEO Rule: Only ONE <h1> per page. Google uses it to know your main topic. Use <h2> for sections, <h3> for subsections.

Paragraphs

<p> tags wrap blocks of text. Browsers add space above and below automatically. Never use <br> to make paragraphs. Use <p>.

Why This Matters

1. Screen readers use headings to navigate. Blind users jump h2 → h2 to skim.

2. Google ranks h1/h2 content higher than plain text.

3. Humans scan headings first. Good structure = people actually read your stuff.

Pro tip: Don't skip levels. Don't go <h1> → <h3>. Always go h1 → h2 → h3. It breaks accessibility tools.

Your Task

In the editor → Create this structure:

1. <h1> with your site name

2. <h2> called "About Me"

3. <p> under it with 2-3 sentences about you

4. Another <h2> called "My Projects"

5. <p> under it describing a project idea
