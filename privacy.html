Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

TL;DR

We don't collect your data. SHIPhtml runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. Your lesson progress and code stay on your device.

Data We Store

SHIPhtml uses localStorage to save your progress. This includes:

  • Which lessons you've completed: lesson-{id}-complete
  • Your theme preference: theme
  • Playground code: playground-html, playground-css, playground-js

This data never leaves your browser. We can't see it. If you clear your browser data, it's gone.

Third-Party Services

SHIPhtml loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address when fonts load. See Google's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts. If we add analytics later, we'll update this page.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to export or delete. To clear your local data: DevTools → Application → Local Storage → Clear, or clear browser data.

Hosting

If deployed, SHIPhtml may be hosted on Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages. Those platforms may log IP addresses for security. Check their privacy policies.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or email: privacy@shiphtml.dev
